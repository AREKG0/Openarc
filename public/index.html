Our Privacy Mission

We believe in a future where artificial intelligence respects your digital autonomy. Openarc is built entirely around local privacy.

"Providing free local AI with zero data training on your data, surveillance-free, with no third parties involved, and for the betterment of privacy."

- No Data Leakage: Your prompts, documents, and images never leave this machine. Zero external API calls.
- Zero Training: Local models do not upload or use your inputs to train or improve future model weights.
- Surveillance-Free: No analytics trackers, no user telemetry, and no diagnostic logs sent to servers.

What's New (v2.1)

• Self-Healing Table Rendering: The UI now dynamically intercepts and auto-corrects broken Markdown tables stream-by-stream, rendering them in a beautiful, styled table format.
• Lightweight Model Guard: When running smaller models (like Qwen 2.5 3B), the system prompt is automatically condensed to conserve context and prevent rule leakage.
• Ollama Auto-Reconnector: The backend now periodically polls for Ollama's availability, allowing the app to automatically load as soon as Ollama is launched.
• Absolute Local Privacy: Tavily Web Search has been completely removed to guarantee that all chat actions and documents stay 100% local and tracking-free.

Our Mission

We believe in a future where artificial intelligence respects your digital autonomy.

"Providing free local AI with zero data training on your data, surveillance-free, with no third parties involved, and for the betterment of privacy."

- No Data Leakage: Your prompts, documents, and images never leave this machine. Zero external API calls.
- Zero Training: Local models do not upload or use your inputs to train or improve future model weights.
- Surveillance-Free: No analytics trackers, no user telemetry, and no diagnostic logs sent to servers.
